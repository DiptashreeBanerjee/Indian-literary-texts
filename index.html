Extracting Flora and Fauna from Indian Texts

This project compiles the plants (flora) and animals (fauna) mentioned in five books on Indian nature and culture: Cities and Canopies, Indica, Rain in the Mountains, Sacred Plants of India and The Fall of a Sparrow. The results for each book, and for all five together, can be explored from the Project menu above.

Limitations of the Project

  • Curated lists: The lists were compiled from the books which was chosen through Convenient Sampling.
  • Discrepancy in the name: The lists produced had several shortcomings which were not attempted to get the desired result but rather acknowledged as a limitation.
  • Temperature differrence: For experimentation various temperatures were tried on, before going with the final results.
  • Overlap between books: The combined list contains duplicates as they were not removed..
  • Not all entries are living species: Items such as jaggery, ghee and gram flour are food products, and Indica lists fossil and prehistoric animals.
  • Spelling variations: Some names may contain typographical errors and need checking against the books.
  • No frequency data: The lists show which species are mentioned, not how often.
