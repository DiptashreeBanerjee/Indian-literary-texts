02 · About

The five books

The project compiles the plants (flora) and animals (fauna) mentioned in five books on Indian nature and culture: Cities and Canopies, Indica, Rain in the Mountains, Sacred Plants of India and The Fall of a Sparrow. Select a count to see what was extracted from each.

04 · Limitations

Limitations of the project

Curated lists

The lists were compiled from books chosen through convenient sampling.

Discrepancies in names

The lists produced had several shortcomings. These were not corrected but acknowledged as a limitation.

Temperature differences

Various temperature settings were tried during experimentation before going with the final results.

Overlap between books

The combined list contains duplicates because they were not removed.

Not all entries are living species

Items such as jaggery, ghee and gram flour are food products, and Indica lists fossil and prehistoric animals.

Spelling variations

Some names may contain typographical errors and need checking against the books.

No frequency data

The lists show which species are mentioned, not how often.
